There’s No Such Thing as a Free Lunch… Except in Germany!

One weekend, when I was living in Europe, I decided to visit a small town in Germany called Ludwigsburg, near Stuttgart.

Why Ludwigsburg?

Honestly, I have no idea.

I saw some pictures online, thought it looked nice, and decided to go. I even knew people from Stuttgart who had never been there.

So I took the train and arrived in this little town. Before I opened the map, I noticed a crowd of people walking in the same direction.

I thought, “Well, if everyone’s going there, there must be something interesting.”

So naturally, I followed them.

Eventually, I found what looked like a cafeteria. There was food, there were people, and I was hungry. Perfect!

I ordered by pointing at the food I wanted, and everything was going great.

Until I tried to pay.

I pulled out my credit card, but the cashier shook his head. He didn’t speak English, so he went to find another staff member who did.

Apparently, they only accepted student cards.

Student cards?!

I said, “I don’t have a student card. I’m from America. I only have a credit card!”

When the second guy heard I was from America, he looked completely shocked.

“America?! What are you doing here?”

Then he stared at me for five seconds, laughed for a little bit, and said, “Don’t worry. Lunch is on us!”

Apparently, everyone else knew exactly where they were going. I was the only person who had followed the crowd without knowing why.

So what did I learn? Following strangers can sometimes lead to a pretty good lunch!
