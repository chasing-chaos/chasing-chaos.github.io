I fear my right leg has become too powerful

Went to ballet class this morning half asleep.

Normally, after finishing one side at the barre, you either turn around OR switch to the other barre to work the opposite leg.

Apparently, my half-asleep brain decided to do BOTH. EVERY. SINGLE. TIME - Tendus, dégagés, rond de jambes, développés, arabesques, attitudes, frappés. All on my right leg.

Somehow, not a single question crossed my mind.

Then we moved away from the barre to do grand battements on the floor, and I finally started using my left leg.

I remember thinking, "Wow, my left leg feels so good today! Am I actually getting better at left leg??”

It wasn't until a full 70 minutes into class, that it finally hit me.

I HAD BEEN TRAINING THE SAME LEG THE ENTIRE TIME.

My right leg went through an entire ballet boot camp. My left leg did nothing. And I genuinely thought I was making progress.

After class, I went up to my teacher and said, "I have something funny to tell you."

The look of concern on her face was immediate. Like she already knew I was about to say something chaotic.

And mind you, I'm a regular in her class. She knows I've done ballet before.

I told her what happened.

She just shook her head in disbelief. I think she was questioning everything she’d ever taught me.
