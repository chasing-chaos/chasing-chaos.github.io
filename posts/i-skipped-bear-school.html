Chasing Chaos
July 14, 2025

I Skipped Bear School. Then I Ran Toward Three Bears.

My husband and I went to Alaska to see bears at Katmai National Park.

We spent over $1,000 per person to get there from Anchorage, and we only had two hours to see the bears.

We wanted to go to bear school first, but we got lost trying to find it. I was getting frustrated because we were running out of time.

I said, “Forget bear school! I came here to see bears, not go to school.”

So my husband went to bear school, and I went straight to the waterfall to watch bears catching salmon.

Now, here’s something important. The number one rule at bear school is: never run around bears.

My husband knew that.

I didn’t.

There was a 30-minute wait to get to the best viewing spot. When it was almost our turn, my husband still wasn’t back.

There was no cell service, and I didn’t want him to miss our turn. So I went looking for him.

I was walking along an empty trail when I finally spotted my husband.

I was so excited that I started running toward him, shouting his name.

What I didn’t realize was that there were three bears nearby.

My husband saw me running.

He saw the bears.

And he nearly had a heart attack.

Suddenly, two huge bears came out of the bushes, just two or three meters in front of me.

I completely froze. I was terrified.

Luckily, the bears didn’t even look at me. They just went off in another direction.

When I finally reached my husband, I said, “Oh my God! I almost got eaten by bears!”

And he said, “You were running! That’s the first thing they tell you not to do at bear school!”

In the end, we made it to the waterfall and saw bears catching salmon. We even saw a mother bear with her cubs. It was amazing.

So, what did I learn from this trip?

First, bear school is probably worth attending.

Second, my husband went to bear school to learn how to survive a bear encounter, but almost died of a heart attack watching me.

And finally, I love watching bears eat salmon. I just don’t want to BE the salmon!
